Keep your workspace running.

You do not need VS Code. On your laptop, npm start runs the service until you stop it or the laptop turns off. A hosted service stays on its server, so you can open the website and extension without starting your laptop's copy.

What runs on the server?

The meeting bot sends speech to your chosen speech provider, such as Deepgram. Lingo sends the resulting text to your translator, such as Google Translate. It saves originals, translations and pending work, displays captions, and retrieves the recording and final transcript after the call.

Choose a host

Railway: a useful starting point with $5 of trial credits for up to 30 days, then $1 monthly free credit. The trial may end sooner if credits are spent. Use persistent storage and check your remaining credit. Railway trial details.

Render and Koyeb free services: useful for disposable demos. They sleep when idle and cannot attach the persistent disk this workspace needs. Sleeping or losing the database can interrupt captions and erase saved transcripts. Render limits · Koyeb limits.

Oracle Always Free: offers eligible virtual machines and storage, but needs more server administration, available capacity and your own HTTPS setup. Idle machines may be reclaimed. Oracle limits.

For dependable use, budget for a small always-running service and persistent storage. Free hosting does not cover speech and translation charges.

Deploy on Railway

The full Node service needs a persistent volume. This is a deployment checklist; a hosted instance has not been published yet. Use the production settings in deploy/railway.env.example and run npm run check:hosting in the deployed service shell to check configuration without displaying credentials.

  1. Put the source in your own GitHub repository, keeping .env and data private. Create a Railway service from that repository; it uses the included Dockerfile.
  2. Attach a writable persistent volume at /app/data and use one replica. Keep the service awake and set its health check to /healthz.
  3. Generate a public HTTPS domain and enter it as PUBLIC_ORIGIN. Set your provider keys, a strong hosted password and session secret using Railway Variables. Use deploy/railway.env.example as the checklist.
  4. Confirm the image's UID 1000 can write the volume. Railway's documented root-user workaround changes the execution user; the detailed source guide explains this deployment requirement.
  5. Open the hosted workspace and test a short call. In the extension disconnect the local workspace, enter the hosted URL and connect again.

The source download includes docs/HOSTING.md with the deployment, Calendar and Discord setup. No cloud deployment is created automatically. Download the starter kit.

Why can't I change the spoken language?

Your caption language changes the text you read. Spoken language tells the recognizer how to understand the audio. MeetStream currently has no live update operation for that setting. End the recording and start another session with the new spoken language, or select automatic detection when starting a supported provider.
