    Prepare a real meeting

    1. Configure MeetStream and a translation provider above, then test their connections. Deepgram uses your existing MeetStream integration.
    2. For local HTTPS, add NGROK_AUTHTOKEN to .env and run npm start. Copy the printed endpoint.
    3. Per-meeting callbacks are configured automatically. Optionally register the workspace lifecycle URL in MeetStream’s Webhooks page, subscribe to events, and save its signing secret in .env.
    4. Start a live meeting here and admit the accessibility bot. Participant permission and platform settings must allow transcription.

    Live webhooks get a separate random URL per meeting. Slack and Drive sharing happens only when you choose a destination in Export & share.
