From the call to a shared understanding.

Connect the conversation

Paste your meeting link in the workspace, choose speech and translation providers, and admit the meeting bot.

2

Read where you meet

Connect your workspace once, then open the extension on your meeting tab. Choose your language and position the caption overlay where it suits you.

3

Take the words with you

Save bilingual documents, VTT or SRT subtitles, notes, and recordings. Share a file or prepare a Gmail draft.

More languages.
A choice of engines.

Use Google Cloud Translation, Azure, JigsawStack, or the optional DeepL adapter for text translation. Choose MeetStream, Deepgram, AssemblyAI, or JigsawStack for speech recognition.

Speech and translation coverage differ. AssemblyAI live through MeetStream currently supports English; JigsawStack live supports Google Meet only. Check your selected language and provider in the workspace.
